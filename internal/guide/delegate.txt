Delegate work to another agent (a worker such as dun, a cheaper model in the owner's harness) and keep the owner's review of everything it sends as the owner. Para starts no agent and executes nothing; the delegation and the conversation live in your chat with the worker. Para records what changes the work and every outward act.

Rule, said to the worker in the first message: outward actions (messages, comment replies, pushes, publishing, anything sent as the owner) come back to you; the worker does them only after the owner approved them.

1. Record the delegation, for visibility only: `task_assign` with assignee agent:<worker> on the task the work serves; the owner's own `task_delegate` does the same and queues agent work. Put the chat link or session id, and what you asked, in the task's notes (`notes_read`, `notes_edit`).
2. Talk in your harness's chat. Record in para only what changes the work: a blocker (`task_block`), a decision, an outward item, a result.
3. Outward items: when the worker wants to send, post, push, or publish, it tells you in the chat. Stage each one as an item of a dossier for the owner (`proposal_add` or `proposal_revise`: a para-message block for a message or comment reply, a para-action block for a push, commit, or publish, a para-doc-edit block for a document edit), with evidence. Bundle related items. Give the owner the review URL.
4. On approval (`proposal_response`), tell the worker in the chat to execute that item, as approved and nothing more. A denied item is not done; tell the worker the owner's reason.
5. Record the result: `proposal_executed` with the URL, by the worker's actor name, and on_behalf_of owner when it was sent as the owner. The receipt names you as the recorder and the worker as the one who did it.
6. An act the owner allowed without review (a local commit the owner said the worker may make): record it after with `action_record` (url, by the worker, on_behalf_of owner). It lands in receipts unchecked for the owner to verify or mark wrong.
7. Results inside the vault: write them with the usual ops (`task_update`, `notes_edit`, `task_resolve`), with sources.

A worker with para MCP of its own may stage its items itself with `proposal_add` (its actor name is on the dossier and the receipt), and report with `proposal_executed`; the rule is the same. A worker that needs only policy and search runs para MCP read-only: para mcp --vault <vault> --actor <worker> --read-only lists only tools that change nothing (`rules_in_effect`, `policy_show`, `search`). By default the worker needs no para at all.

Para cannot stop an agent from sending. It notices afterwards: a sweep's scan_out passes acts it cannot match as outward on `sweep_record`, and `health` names each one with no approved item and no record (unreviewed_outward). Record it with `action_record`, or tell the owner.

Example: the owner asks you to have dun reply to the PR review comments on repo acme/portal. You assign the task to agent:dun and tell dun in the chat: read the comments, draft each reply and the fix, send nothing; commit locally is fine (the owner said so), no push. dun returns three replies and a commit. You stage the three replies and the push as one dossier (para-message items, one para-action item), and record the local commit with `action_record` by dun. The owner approves two replies and the push and denies one reply with a reason. You tell dun to post the two and push; dun gives the URLs; you record each with `proposal_executed` by dun, on_behalf_of owner, and tell dun the third was denied and why.
