Delegate work to another agent (a worker such as dun, a cheaper model in the owner's harness) and keep the owner's review of everything it sends as the owner. Para starts no agent and executes nothing; the delegation and the conversation live in your chat with the worker. Para records what changes the work and every outward act.

Rule, said to the worker in the first message: outward actions (messages, comment replies, pushes, publishing, anything sent as the owner) come back to you; the worker does them only after the owner approved them.

1. Record the delegation, for visibility only: `task_assign` with assignee agent:<worker> on the task the work serves; the owner's own `task_delegate` does the same and queues agent work. Put the chat link or session id, and what you asked, in the task's notes (`notes_read`, `notes_edit`).
2. Talk in your harness's chat. Record in para only what changes the work: a blocker (`task_block`), a decision, an outward item, a result.
3. Outward items: when the worker wants to send, post, push, or publish, it tells you in the chat. Draft each one as an outgoing draft for the owner (`io_add` direction out: a message to a person or group for a message or comment reply; an action to the names where it lands, such as a repo or URL, for a push, commit, or publish; the text says what and how), with evidence. A document edit is a draft with medium the system and the edit as old and new text. Tell the owner which drafts wait.
4. When the owner approves a draft (`io_approve`), tell the worker in the chat to do that one, as approved and nothing more. A draft the owner declined is not done; tell the worker the owner's reason.
5. Record the result: `io_send` with the URL or locator, and say in why that the worker did it (and that it was sent as the owner, when it was). The receipt names you as the recorder.
6. An act the owner allowed without review (a local commit the owner said the worker may make): record it after as an outgoing draft with `io_add` direction out, then `io_send`; say in why that the owner allowed it and who did it.
7. Results inside the vault: write them with the usual ops (`task_update`, `notes_edit`, `task_resolve`), with sources.

A worker with para MCP of its own may draft its items itself with `io_add` (its actor name is on the receipt); the owner still approves, and the rule is the same. A worker that needs only policy and search runs para MCP read-only: para mcp --vault <vault> --actor <worker> --read-only lists only tools that change nothing (`rules_in_effect`, `policy_show`, `search`). By default the worker needs no para at all.

Para cannot stop an agent from sending. It notices afterwards: a sweep's scan_out passes acts it cannot match as outward on `sweep_record`, and `health` names each one with no approved draft and no record (unreviewed_outward). Tell the owner, or record the sent text as an outgoing draft with `io_add` direction out, then `io_send`.

Example: the owner asks you to have dun reply to the PR review comments on repo acme/portal. You assign the task to agent:dun and tell dun in the chat: read the comments, draft each reply and the fix, send nothing; commit locally is fine (the owner said so), no push. dun returns three replies and a commit. You draft the three replies and the push as four outgoing drafts, and record the local commit as a fifth draft sent with `io_send`, saying in why that dun made it. The owner approves two replies and the push and declines one reply with a reason. You tell dun to post the two and push; dun gives the URLs; you record each with `io_send`, saying in why that dun did it as the owner, and tell dun the third was declined and why.
