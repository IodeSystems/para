para tracks what you owe and who you wait on.
It keeps goals (bounded outcomes), areas for ongoing responsibilities, tasks with
dependencies and due dates, blockers with reasons, and people.
Plans, docs, and runbooks stay where they live; para links to them.

It is not a documentation system, a long-running engineering plan, a pager,
a calendar app, a general scheduler, or a sync tool. Those stay where they live.
It lists dated work (agenda), exports an .ics, and runs its own recurring work
(tick); it never writes an external calendar.
Alerts stay in monitoring; para keeps the incident record and its follow-up tasks.
