# 0.6.1

## People

- A project can have KPIs: named numbers you track, such as weekly signups or open bugs. Each KPI has a unit, a target, a direction (up: at or above the target is good; down: at or below; hold: within a tolerance of it), and a cadence (how often it is read: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, or every n days).
- Define one with `para kpi add <project> <name> <target> --unit users --direction up --cadence weekly`. Add a reading with `para kpi reading add <project> <name> <value> --source <where you read it>`. A reading needs a source: a URL, a document id, or a path. `para kpi list <project>` shows each KPI with its readings. Change or remove a KPI with `para doc edit` on the `kpis` field.
- Para does not fetch a value. You or an assistant read it with your own tools and record it with its source.
- Health warns when the latest reading of a KPI is off target (`kpi_off_target`), and when a KPI has no reading within its cadence (`kpi_stale`). A KPI with no cadence is never stale.
- The project page shows a KPIs section: each KPI's latest value with a trend arrow, the target, the date and age of the reading, and on, off, or stale. Off and stale KPIs also show at the top of the page. Each row on `/projects` shows how many KPIs are off or stale. The web app shows KPIs only; you add readings on the command line or through an assistant.
- The project file keeps the last 12 readings of each KPI. On the machine named in `request.runner`, tick moves older readings to `archive/<project>-kpis-<year>.md`, one table per project and year. Only that machine writes the archive, so a file-sync tool sees one writer.

## Agents

- New ops through `op_run`: `kpi_add {id, name, unit, target, direction, tolerance, cadence}`, `kpi_reading_add {id, name, value, source, date}`, and `kpi_list {id}`. The core tools do not change. `value` and `target` are numbers. `source` is required: a URL, a document id, a path, or "owner in chat, <date>". Para fetches nothing.
- `edit {id, fields: {kpis}}` replaces the whole list. It checks the same rules: a unique name, direction up, down, or hold, a cadence in the recur grammar, and a dated reading with a source.
- The project bundle (`show {id}` on a project) has two new keys. `kpi_alerts` in the header lists the KPIs that are off or stale, at most 8, then "+N more". `kpis` has one line per KPI, alerts first, at most 8 lines and 600 bytes: `<name>: <value> <unit> <trend> vs <target>; <date> (<age>d); <on|off|stale>`. When lines are cut, `more` says to run `op_run {op: kpi_list}`.
- `list {type: project}` rows have `kpi_alerts`: the count of KPIs off target or stale.
- Health has two new kinds, `kpi_off_target` and `kpi_stale`. A stale KPI's fix names `kpi_reading_add`.
- `para tick` output has `kpi_archived`: `<project>: <n> readings to <archive ids>`.
