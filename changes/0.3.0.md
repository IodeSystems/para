# 0.3.0

## People

- What changed in this release, in short: one rule decides what needs you (Next, the agenda, Today, and the counts read it); every item has Done, Later, and Reopen; dossiers and the review stack are gone (Next is the one place to decide, and an outgoing draft is approved on its own page or card); meetings and commitments are obligations, policies are areas with annotations, and small types fold into the kinds that hold them; agents prepare replies and recurring artifacts, and you approve; an incoming message needs you only when its sender, group, or source matters or a triage rule says so. The vault format goes from 6 to 10 in four steps: 7 obligations (automated), 8 dossiers (automated moves, then a judgement part an agent finishes with `dossier_refile`), 9 policy-docs (automated; optional annotation-placement), 10 small-types (automated; optional notes-placement). On each machine: install this para first and open the vault there once. On one machine: `para vault upgrade --dry-run`, then `para vault upgrade` (refused while another machine seen in 14 days runs an older para; --force overrides); when step 8 waits on its judgement part, have an agent finish it, then run `para vault upgrade` again. `--undo <batch>` reverses a step on the machine that ran it. The page Agent activity is gone: Receipts lists the same writes, with filters.
- A reply an agent sent that answers an incoming message files that message. A triage rule's from matches an address or a name not in your directory, not only a person or group id.
- Investigate next to Done on an open task, obligation, ask, or goal (agenda rows, task lists, the Next card, and the item's page; i on Next): an agent fills in missing details (how we know it is done, how you do it, effort, context with sources), checks whether it is already done and closes it with evidence you can open when it sees it done, and rechecks its assumptions (what it waits on, the due date, who does it, whether it is still needed). The toast has Undo, which cancels the agent work. While the work is open the item shows Agent looking. Near the composer, Investigate and Done first add what you typed in the box (and its files) to the item's notes as a dated entry; Investigate also sends your words to the agent. Undo takes the close or the agent work back and the entry out, while the notes did not change since.
- Activity on every item's page: what happened to the item and what was said about it, newest first. Each row says who (you, an agent by name, the tick, a hand edit, or the person who wrote), when (the exact time on hover), and what: created, edited, state, moved, renamed, each dated entry of the notes, what you asked an agent and how its work ended, messages in and out (with where a reply was sent), and each agent write with why, what changed, and Undo while the document has not changed since. Conversation shows only the notes, the messages, what you asked, and the agents' answers; Everything shows all. It refreshes after the composer saves or sends. It replaces the Agent writes list on the page. `para history <id>` prints the same rows.
- Context at the top of every task, obligation, ask, goal, and question page says what the item is for. Came from: the message or conversation it was filed from (what kind, who, when, and a link to the original), the agent work that made or changed it, and the task it is an action item of. Requested by, Owed to, With: who it is for. Why: each goal it leads to and how (directly, or through the task it is part of or the work it unblocks), with the goal's due date; who it is owed to; the area it keeps up, or that it recurs. An item with none says "No reason recorded: link a goal, say who it is for, put it under an area, or drop it": an agent looks into it on its own (Agent looking; it shares the daily cap of fill-in work and asks once per item), or press Investigate. Health's finding for such an item is "No reason recorded"; a task with someone it is for is no longer flagged.
- A task can say who it is for (requested by): a person or group, or text such as a role. Filing a message into a new task fills it with the sender. On the IO page, "Log a conversation or hand-carried item" records a conversation you had in person or by voice (with, date, summary) or something you carried over by hand from another system (from, date, summary, optional link). Neither needs a link to the original, and both are yours to sort. A conversation with people in your directory is a logged contact; anything else lands in the inbox, to file into a task like any message.
- Task rows, the Next card, and an item's page say when the item was created and last updated ("created 3 days ago · updated today"); the date shows on hover.
- A commitment series with no due date falls due on its start's day, else one cadence from today, not at once. Changing journal.agent moves the open daily journal summary to the new agent.

- Later puts a task, goal, question, obligation, or io item off until a date, until another item is done, or someday. The default views and the counts leave it out until then; a view scoped by a topic or a group lists it with a "later until <date>" chip and Bring back. It returns on its date; Undo in the toast brings it back. `para later <id> --until <date>`, `--after <id>`, or no date for someday does the same at a terminal.
- Topics are tags: tag io items and any vault document where they are (`para tag add socials <id>`); nothing moves. `para tag list` counts each topic's open, needs-you, and put-off items. `para later --tag socials --until 2026-11-09` puts a whole topic off.
- Skip on Next is navigation, stored nowhere: j or Skip moves to the next card at once, and the item comes back the next time you open Next.
- Asked of agents lists everything you sent with Send to agent and what you asked an agent in the chat, when the work did not end in its reply: your words, who has it and since when, how it ended, and the receipts it made; take one back while no agent has it (`para sent list` does the same). Work queued or claimed with no change in a day is stale, and health names it (agent_work_stale). Below it, Queue and sweeps lists what waits for an agent, oldest first, and the last sweep of each source; the Sources page shows the last sweep and a failed one. Agenda shows "N asked of agents open (M stale)". Agent work that ended stuck, blocked, or needs clarification needs you.
- `para help claude` and `para help dun` print the setup for that agent harness, as `para mcp --help-claude` does: MCP servers, the skill, tracked asks, the loop, and status. `/para loop`, alone or as `/loop 30m /para loop`, works the agent queue until it is empty.
- An io item can be put off too: it leaves the reply lane of `order` until its date.
- Evidence you are asked to approve must be checkable: a link, a vault document, or for a private chat item the agent's words on how to find it. Older items without one show "no way to verify", with Ask for a link. An incoming Slack, email, Jira, Confluence, GitHub, or Teams message needs its permalink, and the io page shows it.
- A new document in a change preview shows its fields and its text; diffs draw one line per row.
- `para feedback add` files feedback about para itself as an incoming io item tagged para-feedback.
- A vault upgrade step (format 6) marks the vault for these fields. Install this para on every machine of the vault first, then run `para vault upgrade` once. A para newer than the vault reads it; a para older than the vault's format now refuses it with a message.
- One rule decides what needs you, and Next, the agenda, Today, and their counts read it: open, not put off, not an agent's turn, and your turn. Done and put-off items, and a failed task waiting to retry (health still names it), leave the agenda. An item you sent to an agent leaves every list while the agent has it. An optional meeting counts only when you said you go; the rest fold into one "N optional" line. A task that waits on another open item, an ask that waits on a reply, and a meeting more than order.due_soon_days away are not listed until they are your turn.
- Done, Later, and Reopen on every item row: the agenda (by order and by date, blocked tasks, the inbox), task lists, question lists, and a document's page. Done on a question or an io item is Drop, with why. Done's toast has Undo: it undoes the close from its receipt. A put-off row has Bring back. Task lists hide done and dropped tasks until Show done; the questions page lists closed questions under Show done, each with Reopen. `para close`, `para later`, `para reopen`, `para show`, `para list`, and `para send-to-agent` take any id.
- Agent writes apply at once when they can be undone: there is no rating, and nothing goes to a Staged writes dossier any more. Each has a receipt; Undo it on the Receipts page or ask the agent for `receipt_undo`. A write that cannot be undone is yours: an agent is refused vault settings (`vault config set` and `unset`, every key), `doc rename`, and archiving an item that other items wait on, and asks you to do it. Writes already staged in a dossier stay there until you decide them.
- An outgoing draft is sent only after you approve its exact text: `para io approve <id>`, or tell the agent in the chat and it records your words with io_approve (its receipt says self-reported). An edit of the draft after that makes it a draft again.
- The vault setting gate.require is no longer read. Health names it while it is set (gate_setting_unused); `para vault config unset gate.require` clears it, and setting it is refused.
- Tick (`para serve`'s timer, and `para tick`) marks a past optional meeting skipped (attend no) when you made no attend choice (unset or maybe) and it is still scheduled after its end. Each has a receipt, actor tick, that undoes it, and tick does not skip that meeting again. A meeting that is not optional, or one you said yes or no to, is never touched.
- Brief before a meeting: the meeting page and its agenda row show who is in it and, per person, the asks you made of them, what they asked of you (asks in, commitments you owe them), open tasks assigned to them or waiting on them, and open questions asked of them; the asks and items it is linked to; and the notes and outcome of the last held occurrence of a recurring meeting. An attendee not in the directory shows as unresolved. A meeting that needs you shows its brief on the agenda at once; other rows have a Brief button. The brief is read from the vault only, the same on every read. `para brief <id>` prints it.
- After a meeting: its page has Notes with Send to agent to file. The agent formats the notes, files decisions as answered questions, action items as tasks (for you or a person), and asks as Asks, then closes the meeting as held. `para send-to-agent <id> --file-notes` does the same.
- Asked of agents is its own page now (/requests, key 8; it replaces the Agent work page): every request, one record each, by status: waits for your approval, queued, an agent has it, done, failed, taken back. Each shows the item, your words, where it stands, how it ended, stale, and the receipts it made. The stack's Asked of agents tab and the old Agent work list are gone; old links to them land on the page. A result you have not opened needs you ("result to check", or "failed: <reason>"): opening the work or its item marks it seen, as does `para request show` at your terminal, Mark seen on the page (one or all), and Done on the request. `para request seen <id>...` marks results seen. Work that ended before this release is not counted.
- An incoming message needs you only when the sender waits for your reply, the sender (or a group they are in) or the source is priority high, a triage rule says so, or it is your own capture. The rest leaves the agenda, the counts, and the needs-you lists: the Inbox lists what needs you first and the rest below, the Digest tab lists the rest, and the day's journal lists that day's under Inbox digest. Nothing is closed by this; file or drop them as before.
- Priority high, normal, or low on a person, a group, or a source, from its page or `para priority set <id> high`. A person with none takes the highest of their groups.
- Triage rules in .para/triage.yaml (triage.rules): match on sender, group, tag, source class or vendor, medium, or subject; actions needs_you, digest, link, tag, mute. They apply as messages come in, and `para triage run` (with --dry-run) applies them to the open inbox. Each change has a receipt naming the rule; undo it and the rule leaves that message alone.
- A source is a class (mail, chat, tracker, wiki, calendar, other) and a vendor, the product's name. Older sources that name a product (gmail, slack, jira, ...) read as before and are not rewritten; `para source list` shows class/vendor.
- Gaps are filled by agents: on each tick, an item that needs you, or an active goal, that lacks what you need to act on it becomes agent work of its own (kind fill-gap): a task with no done-when, no way to do it (link, command, or steps), or no effort; a question an agent can research with no proposed answer; a goal with no brief. Best value order first, at most `gap.daily_cap` (default 10) a day, one at a time per item, and never twice for the same version of an item. The item stays in your lists while the agent works. The agent's writes are undoable, and the result is a result to check. Asked of agents lists this work under Filled in by agents, and its open count leaves it out. It runs only when tick can queue work (`request.surfaces` lists tick); `para vault config set gap.enabled false` turns it off.
- Ready: an item an agent prepared for you shows a Ready chip on the agenda, with what was prepared one click away: the draft reply, the proposed answer, or the plan on the item. Within each group of the order, ready items come before raw ones; the order of groups is the same. Ready means prepared and not changed since: a question with an agent's proposed answer, an outgoing draft with text, a message or an ask to you that an open draft answers (the draft's thread), or an item an agent marked prepared whose text and fields are as they were then (any edit makes it raw again). On each tick, the top `prep.ahead` (default 5) items an agent can prepare that are not ready and have no open agent work become agent work of kind prepare; like gaps, only when tick can queue, and the item stays in your lists. A done prepare is not a result to check: the Ready chip is the result. Approve on an outgoing draft's page approves its exact text (io_approve), as `para io approve` does.
- Next (/next, key 7) is one card at a time: the top of the value order that needs you and can start now. The card shows the title, the group and why it is there, why it needs you, effort, and due; what an agent prepared, inline (a draft reply with Approve and Edit, a proposed answer with Accept and Edit, a plan or fields filled in), else the task's Do it; a meeting's brief; and the item's composer. Done (with Undo), Later, Skip, Send to agent, and Open; keys j or n skip, k goes back to the card skipped last, d done, l later, s or g send to agent, a approve or accept, o open. A skip is kept only while the page is open. The header counts what needs you, how many are ready, and the reply batch. The web app now opens on Next for a key that can decide; a read-only key still opens on the agenda, and the agenda stays in the menu (key 1). The stack links to Next.
- Obligations: meetings and commitments (something you owe someone, and asks made to you) are one kind, obligation, with pages under /obligations (/meetings links land there). Each has a schedule: once (by a date), at (a time), series (each occurrence its own item, the next made when one closes), or ongoing (no date, reviewed on a cadence), and closes with an outcome: done (held), skipped, dropped, declined. The agenda rows do not change. A vault upgrade step (format 7) makes your meetings, commitments, and asks to you obligations in obligations/, with their ids, text, and every old field kept. Install this para on every machine of the vault first and open the vault there once: `para vault upgrade` refuses while a machine seen in the last 14 days runs an older para (--force runs it anyway; that machine then refuses the vault until upgraded). `para obligation add|schedule|attend` at a terminal.
- Dossiers are retired (format 8). What needs you is the Next list: there is no review stack. A vault upgrade step (format 8, dossiers) moves what each open dossier holds to vault items: a question becomes a vault question, with the dossier's tldr, context, and the narrative of its section in the question's context, and its options and option details (a question with for is the vault question it names); a message is its io draft (or a new outgoing draft when it held its text); an action or a doc edit becomes an outgoing io draft with a medium from its subtype and the action's text as the body; an item put off keeps its date, and one that ran after another waits on where that one went. An approval in a dossier is not carried over: approve each draft again (`para io approve`). Each moved item stays in its block with state moved and the id it went to, and the dossier lists where each item went; a dossier with nothing open closes as superseded. A staged vault change (a change block para did not run) is the judgement part: an agent makes it as a direct write or drops it with a reason (`dossier_refile`, `para dossier refile`), and the step is pending, in `para vault upgrade status`, health (upgrade_pending), the orient prompt, and Today, until none is left; then `para vault upgrade` raises the format. Closed dossiers stay as read-only records on their document page. Install this para on every machine of the vault first and open the vault there once: the run refuses while a machine seen in 14 days runs an older para (--force runs it anyway). `para vault upgrade --dry-run` prints the plan per dossier; `--undo <batch>` restores every file byte for byte and removes the items it made, on the machine that ran it.
- Removed web pages: /proposals (the list), /review, /writes (Recent agent writes: the Receipts page lists agent writes). /stack opens Next; /proposals/<id> opens the document page. The stack's Approve, Reject, Ask agent, Undo, Mark done, and the dossier answer and submit controls are gone; Skip, Later, Done, and Send to agent are on Next.
- Questions is a filter on the Agenda (Questions, or /agenda?show=questions); /questions and its key 9 lead there. /today is gone: Today in the search bar opens the Agenda.

Kept: `para proposal list|show`, `para open`, `para proposal executed` (alias of io send for one release), `para stack later|return` (aliases of later and reopen). New: `para dossier refile <dossier> <item> (--result <id> | --dropped <reason>)`.
- An outgoing draft may be an action to no person (a PR, a ticket, a deploy, a document edit): `to` names where it lands, expects none; its send needs a URL and logs no interaction.
- Policy is part of areas now: a policy is an area that follows its source (the summary text and its rules unchanged, the source and its review state under follows), and exceptions, conventions, constraints, and notes are annotations inline in the area body, under the section they are about (or right after a rule), as Obsidian callouts: `> [!exception] ann-ab12 · kind underspecified · by [[person-x]] · review_by 2027-01-31` then the reason. Obsidian shows them as callouts; para reads the header. A header changed by hand so para cannot read it stays as text, and health names it (annotation_broken). The area page shows each section with its annotations, the source it follows, and its exceptions due for review; /policy lands on the areas. An area needs you 14 days before an exception or constraint in it is due for review, as the files did. When the source changes, `para policy refresh` keeps the text before as a version (<id>-v<n>.source) and never rewrites the summary; an annotation that quotes the source and whose words are gone is named by health (annotation_anchor_lost). `para annotate <area> <text> --kind exception --anchor "Retention" --field by=me` and `para annotation list --expiring 30` at a terminal. A vault upgrade step (format 9) makes your policies areas and your exception, convention, and constraint files annotations: an exception goes under its rule when the rule is still there, a convention or constraint in the first area its applies_in names, else at the top of the area; with no area, in a new area Exceptions, Conventions, or Constraints. Each original file moves to archive/folded/ with folded_into, every key and its text kept. Install this para on every machine of the vault first; `para vault upgrade` refuses while one runs an older para. An optional step (annotation-placement) then asks an agent to move each annotation the upgrade left at the top of an area to its section; dismiss it on an area to leave them (health_dismiss kind annotation_at_top).
- Fewer kinds (format 10): a team, a role, and a domain are groups of that kind (a domain group keeps its owner); a logged interaction is a closed io item with its day, people, link, and text (filed when it came in or you only saw it, sent when you wrote it); an incident is a task of kind incident with its severity, start, end, links, and timeline (todo is open, doing mitigated, done resolved), and still comes first in the order. A vault upgrade step (format 10, small-types) folds the files in place: ids, text, and every old key stay, with folded_from naming the old type; undo it on the machine that ran it. Install this para on every machine of the vault first, then run `para vault upgrade` once. /incidents/<id> opens the item's page. - Recurring artifacts: a report, a status message, or a document that recurs is an obligation an agent owes, with what it produces, a template (a note, or the text), and for a message who it goes to. - The journal narrative is one: with journal.agent set, the next tick on a vault at format 7 or later makes the obligation "Daily journal summary", owed by that agent, once per vault. Each day it asks for the summaries of the journals that ended (the day, and the week or month that ended); a day with no events closes skipped, with no agent run. - The agent box ("Tell the agent about ...") is on the page of an ask, a person, a tracked document, and an area, as on a task or question: say what happened and an agent updates the item and the items around it (a person's open asks and what you owe them, an ask's reply and follow-ups, a document's comments and the tasks it implies, an area's items). - Obligations is a main screen: key 0, and on a phone it takes the place of Receipts in the bottom bar (Receipts is in the menu and on key 2). Meetings is a filter there, beside Promises and Recurring; old /meetings links land on it. - An obligation is a hub. Its page lists Before (its prep: tasks and asks whose parent it is, and what it depends on, done or not, each with the date it should be done by), Documents (notes and tracked documents that link it), Serves (the goal or area it leads to), and Came out of it (action items of a meeting, follow-up obligations). A task, an ask, or an obligation can name an obligation as its parent; the action items of a held meeting do.
- Promises come first. In the order, right after what is overdue or due soon, come the obligations you owe people within the next 7 days (order.owed_soon_days) and their prep. Prep falls due before the promise by its effort: half a day of work a day before, a day or more two days before. A booked meeting after today still waits for its day, but its prep rises. Between two items of the same score, the one owed to someone goes first, then the one for a goal, then upkeep.
- Push out, Redirect, and Cancel on an obligation and its rows. Each asks why: the facts on the ground, or the item with a higher priority. With Tell them on (the default), the people it concerns get a draft per channel (by their preferred contact) saying what changed and why, for you to approve; a meeting with a calendar event also gets a draft of the calendar change. Undo takes back the change and the drafts. A promise past its date that nobody was told of shows in health as "tell them now".
- Idea pools. Keep a good idea for later with a title and a sentence (`para idea add <pool> <title>`, or Add idea to pool on the new Pools page in the menu): it is put off someday with the pool's tag. You write the pools in .para/pools.yaml, each with a trigger: a date (with recur such as quarterly), an open obligation, task, or goal with a tag within N days (an obligation tagged hackathon within 14 days), or an item that closes. When a pool fires, Next and the Agenda show one card, "Hackathon: pick 1 of 6 ideas", the ideas ranked by score; they do not flood Next. Pick brings the checked ideas back and makes each one prep of the obligation that fired the pool, so it shows in that obligation's hub; Skip this round passes it. Either way the pool asks again only at its next instance. An agent fills in the ideas (fill-gap) when the pool fires.
- Publish a chosen set of documents as plain markdown files, for people and agents that do not run para: `para publish set <id> <set>` puts a document in a set (only you can; an agent's change of it is refused), `para publish dir <set> <path>` names the set's folder on this machine (kept in $PARA_HOME, never in the vault), `para publish preview <set>` shows what a run writes, and `para publish <set>` writes it: one file per document and an index.md. Links to documents in the set become relative links, others plain text. An embed or block reference of a document outside the set stops the run and nothing is written. Each run lists the people, groups, and io items the documents link, for you to check before you share. - Sent for you, stamped: an outgoing draft has a voice. As you (the default) is what you know: you approve it, and it goes out in your name with no stamp. For you is a message an agent sends without your review (an acknowledgement, a scheduling reply), and its text ends with a stamp that says so: "— sent by <agent> for <you>; not reviewed by <you>" unless you set another. You decide which messages in .para/delegation.yaml, your file: rules that match by medium, source class and vendor, recipients (people, groups and their members, or address patterns), kind (a label the agent sets, such as scheduling), length, and agent. The stamp can differ per rule, per agent (a worker agent with its own wording), for the file, or in the setting io.stamp.
