# 0.3.0

## People

- Later on the review stack: put a dossier item off until tomorrow, next week, or a date you pick. Later only sets when it surfaces: the default view and the count leave it out until its date, but a view scoped by a topic, a dossier, or a group, and the dossier's own page, list it with a "later until <date>" chip and Bring back. It returns on its date; Undo in the toast brings it back. `para defer --item <dossier>#<item>` and `para defer <dossier>` do the same at a terminal.
- Topics are tags: tag dossier items, whole dossiers, io items, and any vault document where they are (`para tag add socials --id <id> --item <dossier>#<item>`). `para tag list` and the topic chips on the stack count each topic's open, waiting, and put-off items; `para stack --tag` and a chip filter the stack. `para defer --tag socials --until 2026-11-09` puts a whole topic off.
- Skip means not in this batch: a skipped card leaves the to-decide view for this visit to the stack, stays to decide with no date, and returns the next time you open the stack (`proposal_stack` batch, `stack_decide` and `question_review` batch with skip). A dossier Submit that decides nothing says "Nothing was submitted" with the reason for each item (`proposal_submit` left), and the N answered counter leaves out skips and comment-only answers.
- The stack and a dossier page show only what needs you: what you answered, sent to an agent, or put off leaves the list; the state chips and Show all items bring it back. A Sent to agent tab lists everything you sent with Send to agent, with its status and the agent's answer; take one back while no agent has it. `para sent list` does the same.
- Asked of agents (the stack tab that was Sent to agent) also lists what you asked an agent in the chat, when the work did not end in its reply: your words, who has it and since when, how it ended, and the receipts it made. Work queued or claimed with no change in a day is stale, and health names it (agent_work_stale). Below it, Queue and sweeps lists what waits for an agent, oldest first, and the last sweep of each source; the Sources page shows the last sweep and a failed one. Agenda shows "N asked of agents open (M stale)". Agent work that ended stuck, blocked, or needs clarification is listed at the top of the stack's needs-you view.
- `para help claude` and `para help dun` print the setup for that agent harness, as `para mcp --help-claude` does: MCP servers, the skill, tracked asks, the loop, and status. `/para loop`, alone or as `/loop 30m /para loop`, works the agent queue until it is empty.
- An io item can be put off too: it leaves the reply lane of `order` until its date.
- Evidence you are asked to approve must be checkable: a link, a vault document, or for a private chat item the agent's words on how to find it. Older items without one show "no way to verify", with Ask for a link. An incoming Slack, email, Jira, Confluence, GitHub, or Teams message needs its permalink, and the io page shows it.
- A new document in a change preview shows its fields and its text; diffs draw one line per row.
- `para feedback add` files feedback about para itself as an incoming io item tagged para-feedback.
- A vault upgrade step (format 6) marks the vault for these fields. Install this para on every machine of the vault first, then run `para vault upgrade` once. A para newer than the vault reads it; a para older than the vault's format now refuses it with a message.
- One rule decides what needs you, and the agenda, Today, the stack, and their counts read it: open, not put off, not an agent's turn, and your turn. Done and put-off items, and a failed task waiting to retry (health still names it), leave the agenda. An item you sent to an agent leaves every list while the agent has it. An optional meeting counts only when you said you go; the rest fold into one "N optional" line. A task that waits on another open item, an ask that waits on a reply, and a meeting more than order.due_soon_days away are not listed until they are your turn.

## Agents

- An unknown input field is refused with the field you likely meant and the tool's fields on one line (* required, a|b values). Load a tool's schema before its first call.
- owner_said lets an undoable safe write apply without minor and obvious; its receipt is self-reported.
- What you saw the owner do (an approval, a merge, a reply), with a locator and a time, closes the item it completes, rated minor and obvious. Do not ask the owner to confirm it.
- Evidence locators must be a permalink (http or https), a vault id or path, or via {source, ref} with verify; proposal_add and proposal_revise refuse others. io_add with direction in on email, slack, jira, confluence, github, or teams needs ref, or via with verify.
- New tools: tag_add, tag_remove, tag_list, feedback_add. proposal_add, its blocks, and io_add take tags; proposal_stack takes tag. Tag existing items instead of withdrawing and refiling them.
- defer and undefer take items [{proposal, item}], a dossier's id, and tag; io items can be put off. Stack state later is new; proposal_list pending leaves put-off items out.
- proposal_stack takes needs_you; sent_list lists what the owner sent with Send to agent (requests of kind sent-to-agent).
- What the owner asks you in the chat that does not end in your reply is request_add kind owner-ask, with owner_said (their words, verbatim): it is claimed by you at once, stays with you when its lease ends, and you end it with request_done. sent_list lists it with sent-to-agent work, with stale, receipts, and counts. request_next claims sweeps first, then what the owner asked, then other agent work. Requests carry claimed_at and active_at. proposal_stack lists requests (work waiting on the owner) in the unscoped view. The prompt loop (/para loop) works the queue until it is empty. `para mcp --channel` answers as a server older than protocol 2026-07-28 (initialize only; server/discover is refused), since Claude Code does not register a channel server on 2026-07-28.
- report_friction only reads; file feedback about para with feedback_add.
- Rows of order, agenda, task_list, question_list, request_list, io_list, and proposal_stack carry needs_you and a reason (io_list and proposal_stack: needs_you_reason). order lists only what needs the owner: goals, agent work an agent has, tasks waiting on another item or a person, and asks waiting on a reply are left out; agent work to accept or that ended needing the owner is listed. agenda lists optional meetings with no yes in optional, not items, and no longer lists failed tasks to retry (kind retry).
